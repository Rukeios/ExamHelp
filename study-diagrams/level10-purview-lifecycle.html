Level 10: Microsoft Purview: retention, insider risk, eDiscovery and audit

Four jobs, one Purview portal: keep or delete data on schedule, catch risky insiders, find content for legal cases, and record who did what.

4. AUDIT: who did what, and when?

Audit (Standard)

Records and searches user and admin activity. Kept 180 days by default.

Audit (Premium)

One year by default for Entra ID, Exchange, SharePoint and OneDrive records. Custom retention policies and more detailed events. Up to 10 years with an add-on licence.

Retention = keep or delete on a schedule, for everyone.
Hold = freeze it for a legal case (eDiscovery). Different from retention.
Insider risk = employees doing risky things. Not outside attackers.
Audit = the log. "Who deleted this file?" = Audit.
