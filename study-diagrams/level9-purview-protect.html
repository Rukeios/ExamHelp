Level 9: Microsoft Purview: trust, compliance and protecting data

Three questions an organization asks: can we trust Microsoft with our data? How compliant are we? And how do we stop our sensitive data from leaking?

1. TRUST: can we trust Microsoft?

Service Trust Portal

Microsoft's audit reports and compliance documents about its cloud. Clue: "evidence," "audit reports," "how Microsoft meets standards."

Privacy principles (the 4 areas)

  • Data control: it's your data; access, change or delete it
  • Data location: you choose where it's stored
  • Data security: encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Data defense: government requests go back to you where possible, and get challenged where the law allows

Older practice questions may list the earlier six: control, transparency, security, strong legal protections, no content-based targeting, benefits to you.

3. PROTECT THE DATA: know it → label it → stop it leaking

Know your data

Sensitive information typesPattern matches: credit card numbers, national ID numbers
Trainable classifiersMachine learning trained on examples, when there's no fixed pattern
Content explorerWhich items are classified or labeled, and where they are
Activity explorerWhat people did with labeled content (e.g. removed a label)

Sensitivity labels

Classify and protect an item: encryption, watermark, header or footer. The protection travels with the item.

One sensitivity label per item. A label policy decides who sees which labels, can set a default, and can require a reason to lower one.

Data loss prevention (DLP)

Policies that spot sensitive info and block risky sharing in email, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and on devices.

Acts where the sharing happens. Users see a policy tip or a blocked action.

Score swap: compliance score = regulations, in Purview's Compliance Manager. Secure score = security posture, in Defender for Cloud (Level 7).
Label vs DLP: the label is a stamp on the document that goes wherever it goes. DLP is the checkpoint that stops it leaving.
One portal: the Microsoft Purview portal holds Compliance Manager, information protection, DLP, insider risk, eDiscovery and Audit.
