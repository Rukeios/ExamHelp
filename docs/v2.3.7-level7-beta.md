# 2.3.7 beta: new teaching formats on SC-900 Level 7

RJ found that retention drops from Level 7 onward. Levels 1–6 teach ideas that most people already have a feel for, while Levels 7–10 are catalogs of look-alike products. This beta tests three formats on Level 7 only. If they help, Levels 8–10 get the same treatment.

## What's added (`data-lessons-beta-7.js`)
The file adds sections to `LESSON_PILOTS[7]` when the page loads. The original sections and their IDs are unchanged.

- **`base` (type `fieldmap`), first section.** The Level 7 products are placed in three zones of a military base: the gate, inside the wire, and inspection and sentries (Defender for Cloud). Each piece has these fields:
  - `kind`: the job on base
  - `base`: the analogy
  - `azure`: the facts, restated from the Level 7 lesson (checked against Microsoft Learn on 2026-10-03)
  - `clues`: the exam clue words
  - `watch`: where the analogy stops being true
- **`clues` (type `drill`), before Check yourself.** 12 situations, each with one correct answer and three distractors, and a reason for every option.
- **`pairs` (type `drill`).** 10 two-option items covering the commonly confused pairs: NSG and Azure Firewall, DDoS Protection and WAF, CSPM and workload protection, secure score and compliance score, Bastion and NSG.

All three are marked `beta:true`, which shows a Beta chip and a feedback line.

## Behavior
- Drills are memory-only (`pilotDrills`), the same as guided practice. They create no attempts, Review entries or XP, and they don't affect unlocks.
- After each pick, the other options lock. The why is shown, and if the pick was wrong, the right answer and its why are shown too. Focus moves to Next.
- The end screen shows the score and the misses. "Redo my N misses" reruns only those items, and "Run it again" reshuffles every item.
- Known side effect: lesson position is saved as a section index, so a learner who was partway through Level 7 may reopen it one section earlier than where they left off.

Test: `t237.py`, run at 1280, 768, 390 and 360 pixels wide, in light and dark mode, and with maximum accessibility settings.
